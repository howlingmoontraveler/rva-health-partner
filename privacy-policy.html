Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 22, 2026

RVA Health Partner ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (rvahealthpartner.com) or interact with our services. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the site.

1. The RVA Health Partner & Asher Health Relationship

RVA Health Partner provides elite strength training, habit architecture, and fitness coaching services. We do not provide medical advice or practice medicine.

All medical services, including metabolic diagnostics, peptide therapy, bloodwork analysis, and clinical oversight, are provided by our clinical partner, Asher Health (a Management Services Organization), and its affiliated licensed medical providers. Any protected health information (PHI) collected during your medical consultation is handled securely by Asher Health in accordance with HIPAA regulations. RVA Health Partner only accesses health information necessary to inform your physical training program, strictly with your explicit consent.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect on the Site includes:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, and telephone number, that you voluntarily give to us when you submit an application or contact form.
  • Derivative Data: Information our servers automatically collect when you access the Site, such as your IP address, your browser type, your operating system, your access times, and the pages you have viewed directly before and after accessing the Site.
  • Tracking Technologies (Cookies & Pixels): We use cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, and other tracking technologies to customize the Site and improve your experience. See Section 3 for more details.

3. Cookies and Meta Pixel Tracking

We use a Meta Pixel (formerly Facebook Pixel) on our website. The Meta Pixel is a piece of code that allows us to measure, optimize, and build audiences for advertising campaigns. It collects data that helps us track conversions from Facebook ads, optimize ads based on collected data, build targeted audiences for future ads, and remarket to qualified users who have already taken some action on our website.

The Meta Pixel may collect data including your IP address, web browser type, page location, and your interactions with our website. This data is transmitted to Meta (Facebook) and may be used by Meta in accordance with their privacy policy.

Consent: In jurisdictions where required by law, the Meta Pixel and other non-essential cookies will only fire after you provide explicit consent via our Cookie Banner. You can revoke this consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect from you to:

  • Process your application for our coaching program.
  • Communicate with you regarding your application, scheduling, and our services.
  • Deliver targeted advertising, newsletters, and other information regarding promotions and the Site to you.
  • Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve your experience with the Site.

5. Disclosure of Your Information

We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations. Your information may be disclosed as follows:

  • Third-Party Service Providers: We may share your information with third parties that perform services for us, such as email delivery, hosting services, customer service, and marketing assistance (e.g., Meta).
  • By Law or to Protect Rights: If we believe the release of information about you is necessary to respond to legal process, to investigate or remedy potential violations of our policies, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of others.
